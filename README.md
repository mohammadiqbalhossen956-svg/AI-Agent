SovereignAuto.ai — CRM Lead Management AI Agent (Privacy-First)

This repository serves as the core infrastructure for SovereignAuto.ai. We build specialized AI agents that automate CRM lead management for small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) in the DACH region (Germany, Austria, Switzerland), with a primary focus on data sovereignty and GDPR compliance.

The Problem: The Privacy Paradox
Businesses in the DACH region are in a difficult position. While they need AI to remain competitive, standard US-based SaaS tools often fall short of meeting strict GDPR regulations, raising valid concerns regarding data security and potential leakage.

The Solution: Sovereign Workflow Intelligence
We have developed a Modular AI Agent Architecture that provides high-level automation without sacrificing data sovereignty.

Tech Stack: We utilize Mistral AI for intelligence, host on EU-based sovereign servers (Hetzner/OVHcloud), and employ Make.com for secure workflow orchestration.

The Workflow: Our agent automates the entire lead lifecycle—from detecting intent and scoring leads to executing updates directly in HubSpot via custom API connections.

Business Impact and ROI
By allowing our AI agent to handle repetitive manual tasks, your team can focus on high-value sales activities rather than manual data entry.

Time Efficiency: Automating the lead lifecycle eliminates hours of manual administrative work every day.
